## 1) Brand voice & content strategy

### Who we sound like

A sharp older cousin in Braam who already got the offer — not a corporate HR account, not a shouting motivation page.

- **Warm, not soft.** We respect how brutal the SA hunt is. We do not baby people.
- **Specific, not motivational.** Rands, PNet, SuccessFactors, Sandton, learnerships, load-shedding interviews. No “believe in yourself” without a tactic.
- **Gen-Z fluent, adult brain.** “Ghosted”, “be so for real”, “the ick” — once, if it earns it. Never a slang pile-on.
- **SA English always.** organisation, specialised, programme, favourite, labour. Never “resume” unless we are roasting the US template.
- **Honest about the system.** Youth unemployment is real. We do not pretend a prettier CV fixes the economy. We *do* pretend less: most silence is a document problem, a targeting problem, or a robot problem — and those we can fix.
- **One idea per video.** One pain. One fix. One CTA.

**Words we use:** ghosted, robot (ATS), 6-second scan, tailor the advert, proof not duties, callbacks, panel, learnership, matric, NQF, PNet, Careers24, CareerJunction, WhatsApp, rands.

**Words we avoid:** hustle culture, girlboss, “crushing it”, “land your dream job” as a promise, American “resume”, “rockstar ninja”, “guaranteed employment”.

**Promise we can keep:** a CV a local recruiter can actually read — ATS-safe, SA format, written like a human.

### Content pillars (mix each week)

| Pillar | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **CV Craft** | Fix the document | ATS, keywords, no ID number, achievements |
| **Interview Room** | Fix the mouth | Weakness, “tell me about yourself”, salary |
| **Hunt Ops** | Fix the system | Tailoring, follow-ups, LinkedIn, applications/day |
| **SA Market Truths** | Build trust | Scams, EE language, US vs SA CV, load-shedding interviews |
| **Proof / Soft sell** | Book the chat | Before/after bullets, “comment CV”, WhatsApp |

**Ratio:** 70% pure tactic · 20% myth-bust / story · 10% direct offer.

**Funnel in every video:**
Hook (pain, 0–3s) → Pattern interrupt (“here’s why”) → One usable fix → Soft CTA (save / comment / WhatsApp).

**Series names (reuse as on-screen stickers):**
- `FIX THIS BEFORE YOU APPLY`
- `RECRUITER WOULD NEVER SAY THIS`
- `SA INTERVIEW, DECODED`
- `SCAM OR REAL?`
- `YOUR CV IS NOT SHY. THE ROBOT CAN’T READ IT.`

### Conversion rules

- Bio (TikTok + IG): `SA CVs that get callbacks. WhatsApp us →` + link.
- First comment (pin it): the keyword + WhatsApp line from the pack.
- Offer ladder in comments/DMs: free Starter CV → Pro R99/mo → Career R199/mo (interview brief).
- Never hard-sell in the first 20s. Earn the CTA.

## 9) Production specs (CapCut)

- **Size:** 1080×1920, 30fps.
- **Length:** 28–40s sweet spot. Never over 50s for this brand.
- **First frame:** full-screen hook text, cream on navy, thick weight. Must be readable muted.
- **Captions:** auto-captions, then style — cream word, gold word on the punch. Safe margins (IG crops).
- **Cuts:** every 1–1.5s. Zoom 105% Ken Burns on stills.
- **Voice:** CapCut “Narrative” / warm masculine or feminine. Slightly faster than default. Pause 0.2s after the hook.
- **Music:** low documentary underscore, -22 to -28dB under VO. No trending comic sounds on scam or interview pieces.
- **End card (1.4s):** navy full frame, gold wordmark `jobreadycv.za`, line `WhatsApp us · link in bio`.
- **Cover / thumbnail text:** 3–5 words, same hook. Do not waste the cover on the logo.
