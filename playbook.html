Stop saying “I’m a perfectionist”
Interview · ~38s · SA INTERVIEW, DECODED
+
 
STOP SAYING PERFECTIONIST
If your weakness is “I’m a perfectionist”, they have already moved on. They are not hunting a tragic flaw. They are hunting self-awareness plus proof you fix things. Use this three-line answer. One: a real, non-fatal weakness. Two: what it broke. Three: what you run now so it doesn’t break again. Example: “I used to rewrite emails ten times and miss the window. I now send a draft in thirty minutes, then edit once.” Honest. Boring. Employable. That is the whole trick.
1. Empty interview chair and a glass of water on a navy table, champagne light from a tall window, faceless silhouette of an interviewer opposite, Sandton office, 9:16, tense and clean.
2. Close-up of a notebook with three handwritten lines: Weakness / What it broke / What I run now. Gold pen, cream paper, dark desk.
3. Phone voice-memo screen (no real name) on a bedspread, earphones, late-afternoon light through curtains — practising answers, faceless.
4. Red stamp “PERFECTIONIST” being crossed out with a gold marker on cream paper, navy background, cinematic macro.
“I’m a perfectionist” is not humble. It’s a default ringtone.
Real weakness. What it broke. What you run now.
Practise it out loud once. Then go sit in the room.

05
Tell me about yourself — not Grade R
Interview · ~40s · SA INTERVIEW, DECODED
+
 
NOT YOUR LIFE STORY
They said tell me about yourself. They did not say start in Grade R. This question is “why you, for this desk, in the next forty-five seconds.” Use Present, Past, Proof, Pivot. Present: who you are professionally. Past: one relevant stretch. Proof: one number. Pivot: why this role. Example: “I’m a junior analyst who just finished a Standard Bank learnership. I built a weekly exception report that cut manual checks by thirty percent. I want to do that for your risk team.” Sit down. You are done.
1. View from behind a candidate sitting opposite a blurred interview panel in a modern SA boardroom, champagne light, navy chairs, glass water bottles, 9:16, cinematic tension.
2. Four cream index cards on a navy desk labelled PRESENT PAST PROOF PIVOT in gold ink, hands arranging them, top-down.
3. Close-up of a mouth and jaw only, slightly out of focus, speaking, dark background, gold side light — anonymous, not identifiable.
4. Lift interior going up a Sandton or Cape Town office tower, anonymous person in a simple blazer holding a slim navy folder, city reflected in the doors.
Tell me about yourself ≠ your autobiography.
Present. Past. Proof. Pivot. Forty-five seconds. Then stop talking.
If you cannot say it in one breath, it is not ready.
