A simple plan for the founder of JobReadyCV ZA

Build trust. Get clients. Stop doing everything yourself.

Your goal is not more posts or more busy work. Your goal is one repeatable machine that turns helpful content into paid CV clients—and turns great clients into reviews and referrals.

The shortcut: do not build TikTok, Instagram, and your website as separate projects. Build one client journey. Every post, DM, website page, email, review, and referral must move people one step closer to buying.

Your one business machine

This is the only path you need to improve. Find where people drop off, fix that one part, then repeat.

Helpful TikTok / Reel→Free CV checklist or WhatsApp→Fast, helpful reply→Clear paid package→Excellent CV delivery→Review + referral
One call to action: every piece of content should say one thing: “Get the free ATS CV checklist in my bio” or “Send your CV on WhatsApp for a review.” Do not make people guess what to do next.

What you are selling

Do not sell a document. Sell confidence, clarity, and a stronger application—without promising anyone a job.

1

CV Check / ATS Audit

A free lead magnet or a low-cost first step. It reduces risk and gets people talking to you.

2

Job-Ready CV

Your simple core package: fixed price, exact deliverables, clear turnaround and revision policy.

3

Interview-Ready Pack

Your premium option: tailored CV + LinkedIn + cover letter or interview preparation.

Your position: “Human-reviewed, ATS-ready CVs for South African job seekers—fast, clear, and tailored to the role you want.” Put your real price, turnaround time, process, examples, and reviews where people can see them.

Your year in three moves

Do each phase in order. Scaling a weak process only creates more problems.

Months 1–3
Make it work

Build the foundation and prove demand

  • Pick one main audience for 90 days. Example: graduates looking for their first office role. Specific beats broad.
  • Fix the website: who it helps, price from, proof, process, FAQs, WhatsApp button, and one clear CTA.
  • Publish 3–5 short videos each week. Make them about real CV mistakes, examples, job-search advice, and client proof.
  • Set up a simple CRM, email follow-up, WhatsApp Business quick replies, and a weekly spreadsheet scorecard.
  • Ask every happy past client for an honest Google review. Aim for your first 20 genuine reviews.

Win condition: You can reliably get leads, convert some to paid clients, and deliver on time.

Months 4–6
Make it repeatable

Turn your best work into a system

  • Each week, answer one real client question. Turn it into a TikTok, Reel, carousel, website FAQ, email, and WhatsApp reply template.
  • Write simple checklists for every repeated task. The third time you do something, record it and create an SOP.
  • Publish two useful South Africa-focused search articles per month, based on actual questions clients ask.
  • Approach campuses, training providers, career coaches, and small creators with a workshop or tracked referral partnership.
  • Hire a part-time VA first for admin and follow-up; hire an editor second; use a trained CV writer only when quality systems are ready.

Win condition: You are no longer the person chasing every lead, editing every video, or copying information between apps.

Months 7–12
Make it compound

Become the trusted local authority

  • Host a monthly live CV clinic. Turn the best answers into short videos.
  • Build a library of role-specific CV examples and job-search guides. Capture email addresses to access the best resources.
  • Create permission-based case studies: the client’s problem, what changed, and their real outcome.
  • Introduce the next offer only after the core CV service is profitable and consistently high quality: group clinic, job-search package, or B2B workshop.
  • Run small retargeting ads only after you know that your landing page and sales process convert.

Win condition: Reviews, referrals, search traffic, content, and partners bring in clients even when you are not posting every day.

Your next 7 days

Do not try to do the whole year now. Finish these seven actions first.

  1. Choose your main 90-day audience and write down your three packages, prices, scope, and turnaround.
  2. Update the website homepage with one CTA, pricing, process, proof, FAQs, and a WhatsApp button.
  3. Create one free PDF: “20 CV mistakes that stop South Africans getting interviews.”
  4. Set up a lead tracker: Name, source, status, last contact, next follow-up, package, value, result.
  5. Ask past happy clients for a truthful Google review and permission to use an anonymous testimonial.
  6. Record ten short videos in one 90-minute session. Post three this week on TikTok and Instagram Reels.
  7. Contact 20 possible partners with one specific offer: a CV workshop, checklist, or tracked referral code.

Use AI and delegation the right way

Use AI to go faster

  • Turn one video into captions, hooks, carousel ideas, emails, and FAQ drafts.
  • Summarise client feedback into common questions and content ideas.
  • Draft follow-ups and partnership outreach for you to review.
  • Help create checklists, templates, and reporting summaries.

Never automate trust away

  • Never invent CV achievements, qualifications, outcomes, or testimonials.
  • Never use fake followers, fake reviews, or fake results.
  • Do not put sensitive client CV data into AI tools without proper consent and privacy care.
  • Keep human review before a CV is delivered.

Your only job this week

Finish the seven-day list. Then choose one number that is weakest in your client journey and improve only that next week. That is how JobReadyCV ZA gets ahead—through focused systems, not more hustle.
