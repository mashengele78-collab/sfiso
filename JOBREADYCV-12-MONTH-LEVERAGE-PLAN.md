# JobReadyCV ZA — 12-Month Time-Leverage Plan

**Mission:** Build JobReadyCV ZA into South Africa’s most trusted fast-track CV and job-application support brand: consistent qualified traffic, a repeatable client-acquisition system, and a small AI-enabled team that delivers excellent outcomes without the founder being the bottleneck.

**One-year definition of success (set your exact numbers before week 1):**

| Outcome | Minimum operating target | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Qualified website visits | 5,000+ / month | A reliable top of funnel |
| Leads | 250+ / month | Enough volume to improve sales and conversion |
| Paid clients | 50+ / month | Proof of demand and operating capacity |
| Lead-to-paid conversion | 15–25% | More valuable than vanity traffic |
| Google / public reviews | 100+ at 4.8+ average | Trust asset that compounds |
| Average order value | R700–R1,500+ | Supports quality delivery and acquisition cost |
| Delivery turnaround | Standardized 24–72 hours | A clear, believable promise |
| Founder dependency | <20% of fulfilment work | The business can scale |

> Replace targets with numbers appropriate to your prices and capacity. Track them weekly; do not optimize followers in isolation.

## The strategic shortcut: build one conversion engine, not three separate brands

TikTok, Instagram, the website, WhatsApp, email, content, and referrals must all feed a **single measurable path**:

**Useful short-form content → free job-search resource / WhatsApp → quick qualification → checkout or consult → excellent CV outcome → review + referral → case study / content.**

The leverage comes from reusing one insight many times and automating every repetitive handoff:

1. Answer one real client question each week.
2. Turn it into a TikTok, Reel, carousel, article, email, WhatsApp follow-up, and website FAQ.
3. Drive every asset to one focused next step—not “follow for more.”
4. Capture the lead in a CRM, then make the right follow-up automatic.
5. Use client outcomes, testimonials, and referrals to increase trust and reduce paid acquisition needs.

### Your defensible position

Do **not** compete on “cheap CVs” or generic AI text. Own a clear promise such as:

> “Human-reviewed, ATS-ready CVs built for South African job seekers—fast, clear, and tailored to the role you want.”

Validate the wording with 10 customer interviews. Make your differentiators visible: actual turnaround, revision policy, examples, secure payment, human review, local job-market knowledge, real reviews, and clear pricing.

## The 90-day sprint: prove the machine before scaling it

### Days 1–14 — Foundation and trust

**Founder-only work**

- Interview 10 ideal customers: recent graduates, career switchers, and professionals who applied unsuccessfully. Ask what stopped them from buying, what words they use, which jobs they target, and what result they value.
- Select **one primary niche for the next 90 days**. Example: graduates seeking their first office role. A narrow message will grow faster than “CVs for everyone.”
- Define 3 productized offers, with fixed scope, price, turnaround, revision terms, and checkout:
  - **CV Audit / ATS Scorecard** — low-risk entry offer or lead magnet.
  - **Job-Ready CV** — core offer.
  - **Interview-Ready Pack** — premium offer: tailored CV + LinkedIn + cover letter / interview prep.
- Write a one-page service standard: what clients submit, exactly what they receive, timelines, escalation, and refund/revision rules.

**Website essentials**

- Make the homepage answer in five seconds: who it is for, outcome, turnaround, price from, proof, and one button: **“Get my CV reviewed”** or **“Order my CV.”**
- Add dedicated pages for each core persona, offer, pricing, FAQs, examples (anonymized), testimonials, contact/WhatsApp, privacy policy, and terms.
- Add GA4 and Search Console; submit sitemap; install Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel only after a consent/privacy review.
- Use UTM links for every bio and campaign so you know which channel creates paid clients.
- Add one simple lead magnet: **“South African ATS CV Checklist + 20 CV Mistakes That Block Interviews.”** Deliver it instantly by email.

**Trust sprint**

- Ask every past satisfied client for a Google review using a direct review link. Target the first 20 reviews ethically—never buy or fabricate reviews.
- Obtain written permission to use anonymized before/after CV snippets and results. Never publish personal data, employer details, or results you cannot substantiate.
- Record three short founder videos: who you help, how the process works, and what “ATS-ready” actually means.

### Days 15–45 — Audience and lead generation

**Content operating system: 4 pillars**

1. **Fixes:** “Three reasons your CV gets ignored.”
2. **Proof:** anonymized before/after rewrites, client process, testimonials.
3. **Opportunity:** job-search, interview, LinkedIn, and application tips relevant to South Africa.
4. **Founder trust:** your standards, behind-the-scenes review process, honest opinions and mistakes to avoid.

Create 3–5 vertical videos weekly (25–45 seconds) and post the adapted version to both TikTok and Instagram Reels. Every video follows:

- **Hook (first 2 seconds):** a painful, specific observation.
- **Value:** one practical correction, demonstrated visually.
- **Proof:** your expertise, an example, or a client result.
- **CTA:** “Comment CHECKLIST” / “Get the free ATS checklist in my bio” / “Send CV on WhatsApp for an audit.”

Batch record for 90 minutes each week. Edit from templates; do not create every post from scratch. Use AI to create variations, captions, subtitle drafts, hooks, and repurposed copy—then review every factual and employment-related claim yourself.

**Distribution shortcut**

- Spend 20 minutes/day responding to comments and DMs with genuinely helpful answers.
- Turn high-intent comments into short video replies.
- Partner with 10 micro-communities that already have your buyers: student societies, training providers, bootcamps, recruitment-adjacent creators, career coaches, and alumni pages. Offer a useful workshop, checklist, or affiliate code—not a vague “collab.”
- Pitch 20 targeted partnership emails per week. Track contact, reply, campaign, leads, and conversions in the CRM.

### Days 46–90 — Conversion and repeatability

- Launch a 5-message email sequence for new checklist subscribers: deliver resource → quick win → common mistake → proof/case study → clear limited-capacity CTA.
- Set up WhatsApp Business greeting, quick replies, labels, catalogue, and a qualification form. Reply to sales inquiries within one business hour where possible.
- Build a simple funnel dashboard and review it every Monday:
  - visits by source;
  - lead magnet conversion;
  - booked/orders;
  - lead-to-paid conversion;
  - average order value;
  - delivery time;
  - refund/revision rate;
  - reviews/referrals;
  - revenue and cost per acquired customer.
- Run one optimization test at a time: headline, CTA, price packaging, proof block, checkout flow, or follow-up. Keep winners, document the result.
- Test retargeting only after the pixel has enough visitors and you have a conversion event. Start small; retarget site visitors and engaged viewers with proof/testimonial creative rather than cold “buy now” ads.

**90-day gate:** Do not scale paid ads or hiring until you can show (a) a repeatable lead source, (b) a healthy conversion rate, (c) on-time quality fulfilment, and (d) a clear gross margin per sale.

## Months 4–6 — Scale what works

### 1. Build a content factory

Turn your strongest weekly customer question into this reusable package:

| Asset | Owner | Time limit |
|---|---|---:|
| One insight + source/example | Founder / CV expert | 20 min |
| 3 short video scripts + 10 hooks | AI first draft, founder approves | 20 min |
| 3 videos and captions | Editor | 2–3 hours |
| Carousel + blog FAQ + email | Content assistant using template | 90 min |
| Schedule + UTM links + report | VA | 30 min |

Maintain a content database with: customer question, pillar, hook, status, link, views, saves, leads, and sales. Double down on content that produces saves, DMs, email sign-ups, and purchases—not just views.

### 2. Win search with a small library of high-intent pages

Publish two genuinely useful, locally relevant pieces each month, based on Search Console queries and sales calls. Examples:

- ATS-friendly CV format in South Africa
- Graduate CV examples for [role]
- How to write a CV with no experience in South Africa
- Professional CV writer pricing: what is included?
- CV vs résumé / cover-letter examples for [role]
- LinkedIn headline examples for job seekers

Each page needs first-hand insight, a clear author, dates where relevant, a downloadable template/checklist, internal links to the relevant offer, and no thin AI filler. AI can speed research outlines and first drafts; an expert must add examples and verify every claim.

### 3. Add structured partnerships

Create three scalable channels:

- **Campus/training partner:** career workshop + tracked referral code.
- **Recruitment/career partner:** a clearly scoped referral arrangement, aligned with their policies.
- **Creator affiliate:** a unique landing page/code and a transparent commission after paid delivery.

Give each partner: a co-branded resource, landing page, tracking link, a short promo pack, and a monthly results update. Cut partners that do not create qualified leads after a defined trial.

### 4. Hire the first leverage roles

Hire only after creating checklists and reviewing sample work.

1. **VA / client coordinator (part-time):** inbox, follow-ups, CRM hygiene, appointment/admin, review requests, partner tracking.
2. **Video editor (freelance):** template-driven editing, captions, exports, scheduling files.
3. **Trained CV reviewer/writer (contract):** only when volume exceeds your capacity; use a rubric and mandatory quality assurance.

Your job shifts to: offer/message, quality bar, top sales calls, content authority, partnerships, and scorecard review.

## Months 7–12 — Compound authority and create scale

### Authority that cannot be copied quickly

- Publish a quarterly **Job Seeker Pulse** based on anonymized survey insights from your audience. This can create PR, backlinks, and trust if methodology is transparent.
- Run a monthly live CV clinic / Q&A on TikTok or Instagram; clip the best answers into content.
- Build a vetted library of role-specific CV and LinkedIn examples. Gate the highest-value templates behind email capture or a paid product.
- Capture a consented case-study pipeline: problem → intervention → client’s actual outcome → permission level. A portfolio of credible outcomes is more persuasive than polished generic claims.

### Product ladder and recurring value

A service business can add leverage without lowering quality:

- low-cost CV audit or template/checklist;
- core done-for-you CV;
- premium job-search package;
- optional group CV clinic or job-search accountability cohort;
- B2B workshops for colleges, bootcamps, and employers supporting displaced staff.

Do not launch all at once. Launch the next product only when the core offer is consistently profitable, on-time, and well reviewed.

### Mature operating cadence

- **Daily (30–60 mins founder):** approve quality exceptions, record an insight, respond to highest-intent leads.
- **Weekly (60 mins):** scorecard; review pipeline, cash, customer feedback, delivery quality, content winners, and one constraint.
- **Monthly (2 hours):** channel profitability, capacity plan, experiment decisions, partner ranking, and customer interview.
- **Quarterly (half day):** stop low-return work, choose the next bottleneck, update offers/pricing/processes.

## Automation stack — use simple tools, not a complicated tech project

Choose tools that your team will actually use; connect them after the manual process works.

| Need | Lean option | Automation to build |
|---|---|---|
| Website & landing pages | Existing site + focused pages | Form submission → CRM contact + source/UTM fields |
| Forms & intake | Tally, Typeform, or Google Forms | Paid order → intake form + document checklist |
| CRM | HubSpot Free, Zoho, or Airtable | Lead stage, owner, follow-up date, lead source |
| Payments | A reputable SA-compatible processor | Successful payment → order record + client welcome email |
| WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business | Labels, saved replies, intake link, response SLA |
| Email | MailerLite, Brevo, or HubSpot | Checklist sequence, abandoned inquiry, review/referral request |
| Workflow automation | Make or Zapier | Form/payment/CRM/email notifications and task creation |
| Delivery/project management | ClickUp, Trello, or Notion | Template task list per order, deadline and QA status |
| Scheduling | Buffer, Metricool, or Meta Business Suite | Approved content queue and weekly performance export |
| Reporting | Looker Studio or a simple spreadsheet | Weekly scorecard from analytics, CRM, and sales data |

**Automate these first:** lead capture acknowledgment, lead assignment, follow-up reminders, payment confirmation, client intake/chasing, delivery status messages, review request, referral request, and KPI reporting.

**Never fully automate:** claims about client results, final CV recommendations, sensitive client-data decisions, complaint handling, refunds, and public replies to serious concerns.

## AI playbook — speed up work without losing trust

### Good uses

- Repurpose a recorded video or client question into hooks, scripts, captions, carousel outline, FAQ, and email draft.
- Summarize customer interview notes into objections, wording, and content opportunities.
- Convert your approved CV-writing rubric into an internal first-draft checklist.
- Classify inbound leads by persona, urgency, job target, and missing information.
- Draft personalized—but reviewed—follow-ups and partnership outreach.
- Analyze anonymized feedback themes and weekly performance trends.

### Required guardrails

- Get explicit consent and protect CVs, contact details, identity documents, and employment information. Do not paste sensitive client documents into consumer AI tools without a suitable privacy/data-processing arrangement.
- Tell clients where AI assists your process, and retain meaningful human review.
- Never invent achievements, qualifications, references, employers, interview outcomes, or testimonials.
- Build an internal CV quality rubric: factual accuracy, role relevance, ATS readability, achievement clarity, grammar, formatting, and client approval.
- Keep a human sign-off before delivery.

## Delegation map — keep yourself out of low-value work

| Keep with founder | Document, then delegate | Eliminate / automate |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning, pricing, standards | Video editing and scheduling | Copying leads between tools |
| Top partnerships and strategic sales | CRM updates and routine follow-ups | Repeating the same DM answers |
| Expert review / quality control | Client onboarding and document chasing | Manual payment confirmations |
| Flagship content and live sessions | First-draft research / repurposing | Individual social posting |
| Hiring and scorecard decisions | Standard CV production after rubric proof | Weekly metric collection |

Use a **“record once, SOP once”** rule. The third time a task repeats, record a short screen video, write a checklist, define the expected output, then delegate or automate it.

## Weekly scorecard and decision rules

Build one page with weekly and monthly trends. Every metric needs an owner and a next action.

| Funnel stage | Metric | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | qualified visits, video saves, profile clicks | Is the message reaching the correct job seeker? |
| Capture | landing-page conversion, WhatsApp starts, email leads | Is the offer valuable and the CTA obvious? |
| Sales | response time, booked calls, lead-to-paid %, AOV | Is trust/pricing/follow-up the blockage? |
| Delivery | turnaround, revision rate, QA score | Is the process clear and capacity sufficient? |
| Retention | reviews, referral rate, repeat/upsell rate | Are clients delighted enough to recommend you? |
| Profit | gross margin, cost per lead/customer, cash collected | Is a channel actually worth scaling? |

**Decision rules**

- If traffic is high but leads are low: improve offer, CTA, landing page speed/message, and proof.
- If leads are high but sales are low: inspect response time, qualification, pricing, payment friction, testimonials, and follow-up.
- If sales grow but delivery slips: pause promotion, fix SOP/capacity/QA, then resume.
- If a post has high views but no leads: treat it as awareness; do not mistake it for a business result.
- If a channel creates profitable clients for 4 consecutive weeks: allocate more creator time, partner effort, or a measured ad test.

## What to avoid

- Buying followers, reviews, fake testimonials, backlinks, or client results.
- Publishing generic high-volume AI articles or pursuing views without a measurable CTA.
- Serving every audience with the same vague offer.
- Scaling ads before conversion, fulfilment, and unit economics work organically.
- Hiring before documenting the task and quality standard.
- Holding client data in scattered personal inboxes or sharing it casually.
- Promising jobs or interview outcomes you cannot control. Sell the quality of your process and materials, not guaranteed employment.

**The one-year advantage is not working ten times harder.** It is turning every client insight into reusable media, every inquiry into an automated and human follow-up system, every great outcome into verifiable trust, and every repeated task into an SOP that someone else or software can execute.
