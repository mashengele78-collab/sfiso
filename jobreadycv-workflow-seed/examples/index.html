Stage 5 of 6

Human QA and export gate

A human compares the draft with the source and vacancy. The optional Human Review tool may collect visual edits and comments, but it cannot approve its own output.

✓ Facts match confirmed source
✓ No invented qualification
✓ Unsupported metrics removed
✓ Standard section headings
✓ One-column reading order
✓ Relevant terms used truthfully
✓ Contact placeholders checked
✓ Human delivery approval recorded

Export checks represented in the real workflow

  • Open DOCX and PDF independently.
  • Check clipping, page breaks, blank pages and missing glyphs.
  • Copy PDF text to verify a usable text layer and reading order.
  • Check filenames, links and metadata for cross-customer leakage.
!
This demo does not render DOCX or PDF. Those checks remain manual until a separately audited renderer is justified.
